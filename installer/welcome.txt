NEXVARY USB Studio 0.7.0

USB modem grouping, user-initiated AT and SIM diagnostics, network status,
SMS (including received UCS2/GSM7 PDU), consented OS-managed data connection,
and redacted reports. Full-color Oxygen icons and Arabic RTL desktop.

New: offline integration with NEXVARY SIM Manager 0.922.0 for LPA activation
QR handoff and imported phone recycling CSV reports. Activation credentials
are not persisted or sent to a server. eSIM provisioning requires an
authorized LPA on the phone; this desktop does not download profiles.

Private consent-gated AKA adapter and WiFi-Call contract are implemented,
but live AKA and carrier calls remain unverified. Hardware support varies.
No Ki/OPc extraction, firmware flashing, automatic driver installation,
SIM writes or file associations.

Upgrades and uninstall preserve user settings and reports.
